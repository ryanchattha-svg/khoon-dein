Blood Fridge Access

Two-step security: your NFC card + a one-time code

Get your one-time code

Your card must be registered by the admin first. The code works once, only with your card, and expires in 10 minutes.

Your details are checked against the registered card. Codes use only the keys that work on the fridge keypad.

Your one-time code
  1. Walk to the fridge and tap your NFC card.
  2. When the screen says Card OK! Enter Code, type the code within 30 seconds.
  3. Press * to enter. Press 0 to clear.
123456789*0#
Keypad: only the left two columns work.
